## If you fork

You may fork. The licence says so and nothing here narrows it. Two requests, one of which
is a licence condition and one of which is not, and the difference is stated on purpose:

1. **Keep the notices.** The copyright and permission notices in `LICENSE` must travel
   with any copy or substantial portion of the software. That one is a condition of the
   licence, not a courtesy.
2. **Do not ship your fork under this project's name.** If you publish, package or sell a
   modified version, give it your own name, make clear on the first screen of your
   documentation that it is an independent fork, and link back to this repository. Using
   the project's name for a build the maintainer did not publish misrepresents who is
   answering for what that build does to a production tenant, which, for a tool that holds
   write scopes in a directory, is not a naming quibble.

You may always say, truthfully, that your product is "based on entra-tripwire" or
"a fork of entra-tripwire". That is accurate description and it is welcome.

Licence imposes, and in most jurisdictions it is also a copyright matter independent of
the licence.

If you believe someone has done this, you do not need to do anything; the maintainer would
rather hear about it once than have ten people try to handle it. Write to the address
