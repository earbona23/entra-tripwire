# Sponsors

This page is thanks. It is not terms, and it deliberately makes no statement about what is
or will be covered by which licence — that belongs in one place, in the words that actually
govern it: **[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)**. What is in this repository, what is in the paid
module, and what a licence change could and could not do are all answered there.

What sponsorship pays for is the part with an ongoing cost: keeping the rule catalogue
current, tracking the Microsoft Graph and platform changes that quietly break a check,
answering issues, and cutting the releases that follow. Tiers, one-time amounts, custom
amounts and what each one includes are listed at https://github.com/sponsors/earbona23.
Sponsoring at or above the Pro monthly price (**$49** for Solo) covers a licence key; email after sponsoring so
it can be issued to the right organisation name — see [docs/PRO.md](docs/PRO.md).

Anyone credited here can ask to be removed, or to have their entry changed, at any time:
**earbona@arrankago.com**. Nobody is added without asking first.

Sponsors on a named tier are credited in this file, most recent first, from the first
release after they sign up. If you would rather not be listed, say so and you will not be.
