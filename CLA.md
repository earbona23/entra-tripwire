## How to accept

Open your pull request. A bot comments on it with a one-click acceptance link, recording
your GitHub account, the version of this document, and the date. Acceptance is once per
person, not once per pull request, and it covers your future contributions to this
Project under the version of this document in force when you accepted.

If the bot is not available, write the following as a comment on your pull request, which
has the same effect:

```
I have read the entra-tripwire Contributor Licence Agreement version 1.0
and I accept its terms for this and my future contributions.
```

Every commit must additionally carry a `Signed-off-by` line certifying the Developer
Certificate of Origin. See `DCO` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
