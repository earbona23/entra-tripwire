      How to read this

      Each decoy is an identity, application or credential that no legitimate process has any reason to touch. A touch is scored from four weighted signals — the purity of the decoy, how unusual the origin is for this tenant, how many distinct decoys the same actor walked inside the correlation window, and the hour — which sum to a confidence out of 100.

      Confidence is a weighted score, not a probability. A signal that could not be evaluated contributes zero and the remaining weights are never renormalised upward, so a degraded score is a lower bound on the evidence rather than a measurement of it.

      Suppressed touches are listed with the rule that suppressed them. A suppression rule that hides evidence is indistinguishable from a bug that loses it.
